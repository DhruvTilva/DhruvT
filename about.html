About Me

Engineer. Creator. Explorer.

Hello, I'm Dhruv

Hi, my name is Dhruv Tilva, and I work as a DevOps engineer in San Francisco.

I've always loved building things. Growing up on a small farm, I had plenty of time and space to explore – whether that meant playing outside in the dirt, building with Lego, designing worlds in Minecraft, or, eventually, writing code. I started programming when I was 12, guided by my brother, who set me up with the tools I needed, helped whenever I got stuck, and even hosted my first websites so I could share them with friends.

Outside of coding, I love spending time outdoors – camping under the stars, soaking in the coastal air, or unwinding around a campfire. I also enjoy walking through the city's scenic streets, playing board games with friends, and indulging in great food.

One day, I'd love to start my own technology business. I don't know exactly what that will look like yet, but I know I want to build something meaningful. Until then, I'll keep learning, creating, and preparing for whatever comes next.
